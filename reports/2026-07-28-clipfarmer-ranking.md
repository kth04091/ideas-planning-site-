## 🥇 Top 3 Contents to Edit TODAY

### 1. "Ranking the Most RELATABLE Daily Struggles 😩" 
**Why now:** Relatable content — **office struggles, gym procrastination, late-night overthinking** — is among the most popular YouTube topics right now, riding the summer shift toward authenticity. A ranked "which one hits hardest" Short is copyright-safe (your own footage / stock / AI b-roll / text cards) and massively shareable because viewers tag friends who "are #1."
- **Format:** Top 5–7 countdown of relatable struggles, 3–4s each, most universal at #1.
- **Title/hook ideas:** "Ranking life's most relatable struggles 😩", "#1 is way too accurate", "If #1 is you, we're the same person".
- **Editing notes:** Text-card + b-roll per struggle, Hormozi captions, relatable/emotional audio bed; end on "which one are you? 👇". 100% safe, endlessly repeatable series.

### 2. "Ranking the FUNNIEST Streamers of 2026 🎮" 
**Why now:** Streamer content is red-hot (IShowSpeed, Kai Cenat, etc.), and a **tier-list of streamers** — built from **faces/logos + your commentary** rather than raw clip dumps — is far safer than reuploading their footage while still riding their search volume. Streamer rankings are elite comment bait ("how is [X] not #1?!").
- **Format:** S/A/B/C tier-list or Top-N of the funniest streamers, 3–4s each, most-debated last.
- **Title/hook ideas:** "Ranking the funniest streamers of 2026 🎮", "If Speed isn't S-tier we riot", "Funniest streamers — tier list".
- **Editing notes:** Streamer photo/logo + tier letter slam-in with SFX; deliberately rank a fan-favorite a tier "too low" to farm comments; end "agree? 👇". Photo/commentary based = far lower claim risk than clips.
- **⚠️ Note:** if you DO use short clips, cut to 2–4s, add your reaction voiceover, crop 9:16, and stamp your ranking overlay — a claim is still possible, so prefer the photo tier-list for safety.

### 3. "Ranking Every Spider-Man Movie — 3 Days Until Brand New Day 🕷️"
